Internet Archive copy of Newman, Glamorgan (1995): the catalogue description is a children's story blurb

🙈 The label

The catalogue description of the Internet Archive copy: “A very hairy but not-too-scary monster story. One dark, stormy night, Owl, Mouse and Rabbit are afraid. Could it be the Monster of the Woods knocking at the door?”

👀 What was there

The item is John Newman’s Glamorgan (The Buildings of Wales, 1995), a 717-page architectural guide with entries for Llandough-juxta-Penarth (pp.378–379), Leckwith (pp.370–371) and Cogan.

The summary note belongs to a children’s picture book. Searched by its description, the guide would not be found.

Glamorgan Archives DBDT/79/1: catalogue description corrected after the original was examined (14 September 2026)

🙈 The label

Catalogue entry: “Lease (counterpart) of land and premises situate in Pembroke Terrace, Cardiff, from the Marquess of Bute to Mr John Williams, May 1877.”

👀 What was there

Examined at our request, the original is a counterpart lease of 2 May 1877 from the Trustees of the Will of the late Marquess of Bute to Mr John Williams of Newport. Glamorgan Archives: “The catalogue description for DBDT/79/1 had a few small errors which have now been corrected.”

A physical check of the original, asked for on 26 August 2026, found the catalogue wording wrong. The deed concerns Pembroke Terrace, Cardiff; the archive found no mention of Llandough in it.

How this page is kept

The page has its own small database, peekaboo.json, pulled from the central evidence catalogue: every record tagged “peekaboo”, plus records picked by hand, with only the fields shown here. A new tagged record appears at the next sync. The notices are in notices.json. Agents can read both files, or use the page’s WebMCP tools (peekaboo_list, peekaboo_notice).
